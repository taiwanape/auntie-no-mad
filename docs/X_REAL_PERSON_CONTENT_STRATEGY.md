# X Real-Person Content Strategy

Updated: 2026-05-29

This is the operating rulebook for the `@auntienomad` X account. It keeps the account away from collage-style output, ecommerce poses, and empty slogans.

## Positioning

`阿姨別生氣` on X is an editorial, topical, real-person character account: smart, spicy, suggestive, and useful.

The account is not an ecommerce channel and should not look like a product ad. Every post needs a topic, a hook, a source, and an Auntie-style point of view.

## Character Lock

- Real-person, photorealistic adult woman.
- Short dark curly hair, gold hoop earrings, leopard-pattern outer layer, black outfit, pink heart accent.
- Curvy body, confident gaze, mature and witty mood.
- Sexy can be stronger on X, but keep it tasteful: no nudity, no explicit sex act, no cheap lingerie-ad framing.
- Do not reuse the same pose repeatedly. Generate new scenes while preserving the same face, body type, wardrobe language, and attitude.

## Visual System

- New AI-generated raster image for each major visual. Do not collage the user's reference images.
- Yellow brand background with halftone, warm lighting, and enough negative space for text.
- Layout: black rounded pill, huge heavy black headline, thick white stroke, black shadow, pink emphasis keyword.
- Avoid generic model cards, character sheets, pixel sunglasses, product-holding poses, and hand-held prop concepts.
- X post images should be bold at 16:9, with the character large enough to feel present and the typography readable on mobile.

## Content Pillars

- Heat translation: take a news item or hot topic and translate it into plain Taiwanese-style commentary.
- Tech and AI: supply chain, chips, scams, platform changes, privacy, and weird internet consequences.
- Money caution: ETFs, market heat, fees, and fraud signals, without giving investment advice.
- Life radar: transport, weather, consumer rules, public services, and daily survival information.
- Meme response: use internet jokes only when there is a clear point or useful context.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with a sharp hook, not a summary.
- Use one strong Auntie translation line.
- Keep finance posts informational and avoid buy/sell/hold advice.
- Cite source names in the post text or image when the claim is news-based.
- Do not post hollow lines like "today cannot lose" unless tied to a real event and punchline.

## Launch Cadence

- Daily anchor: one sourced visual post with the real-person Auntie character.
- Daily lightweight touch: one text-only quote, reply, or short thread tied to the same topic cluster.
- Weekly pattern: two tech/AI posts, two money caution posts, two life radar posts, one meme or culture post.
- Each visual post should use a fresh generated pose or scene while preserving the same character identity.
- Do not post a sourced claim unless the source URL, source name, and date are available in the working notes or post asset.

## Post Gate

Before posting to X, confirm:

- It has a real topic or current hook.
- The visual is newly generated or a project-approved raster fallback, not a reference-image collage.
- The character is consistent but not the same pose again.
- The copy does not sound like ecommerce, sales, or product display.
- The image uses the heavy headline system and remains readable on mobile.
- Finance language is informational and does not tell people what to buy or sell.
